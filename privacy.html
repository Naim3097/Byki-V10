Privacy Policy

BYKI · Effective 19 August 2026

This policy explains how the BYKI mobile application (“BYKI”, “the app”) handles your information. It applies to the Android application published as my.byki.app.

Summary: BYKI does not transmit your personal data to us or to any third party. Everything the app records — your vehicles, scan results, reports and settings — is stored only on your own device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. BYKI has no user accounts in this release and no backend service that receives your data.

The app creates and stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Vehicle details you enter, such as make, model and registration.
  • Diagnostic results read from your OBD2 adapter, including fault codes, system scan results and live engine data.
  • Inspection and health-scan reports you generate.
  • Service records, bookings and shopping cart contents.
  • App preferences and settings.

This data remains on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. We have no ability to access it.

Device permissions and why we need them

Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, and legacy Bluetooth permissions)

Used to discover and connect to your OBD2 diagnostic adapter and to read vehicle data from it. BYKI communicates only with the adapter you choose to connect to. Vehicle data read over Bluetooth is stored on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Location permission is required for two reasons:

  • Bluetooth scanning. Android requires apps to hold location permission in order to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. BYKI needs this to find your OBD2 adapter, even though it does not use your location for that purpose.
  • Location features. Finding nearby workshops and attaching your position to an emergency assistance request.

Your location is used on your device to serve these features and is not transmitted to us or stored on any server. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings; Bluetooth scanning and location-based features will stop working, but the rest of the app continues to function.

Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Used to load images and content shown in the app, and to open external links such as maps, phone calls and WhatsApp when you tap them.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a request, the app uses the Android system photo picker. BYKI can access only the specific images you select, and those images stay on your device.

Leaving the app

BYKI can open other apps and websites at your request — for example a phone dialler, WhatsApp, or a map application for directions to a workshop. Once you leave BYKI, the privacy policy of that other app or website applies. We are not responsible for how those services handle your information.

Third-party services

This release of BYKI does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services of any kind. No advertising identifier is collected. No third-party account sign-in is active in this release.

Children

BYKI is a vehicle diagnostic tool intended for vehicle owners and motor trade professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Revoke Bluetooth or location permission at any time in Android Settings.
  • Delete all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling BYKI.

Because we hold no copy of your data, deleting it on your device deletes it entirely.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of BYKI collects or transmits data — for example if user accounts are introduced — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
